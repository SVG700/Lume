Why LUME Exists

A small interaction problem with a huge cumulative impact

Millions of daily insertions are interrupted by orientation confusion — LUME turns that small frustration into a reliable, fast interaction.

Hidden Friction

USB connectors look symmetrical, but only one orientation works, causing repeated insertion attempts.

Universal Pain Point

Students, professionals, children, and elderly users all face orientation confusion in everyday workflows.

Low-Light Challenge

The experience worsens in low visibility, where the connector direction is hard to identify quickly.

How LUME Works

A simple three-step orientation experience

Fast comprehension, clear LED guidance, and confident first-try insertion.

Step 1

Insert LUME

Place LUME near the USB port like a standard pen drive.

Step 2

LED shows the correct orientation

The LED-facing side quickly signals the right orientation to use.

Step 3

Plug in instantly without flipping

Insert confidently on the first attempt with minimal trial and error.
